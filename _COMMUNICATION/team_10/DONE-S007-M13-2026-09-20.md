against `section-nav.php` — not the generated "{label} — עמוד ראשי" pattern M-12's own

  hand-coding them. "home" skipped on desktop — per your correction, the logo already

  `theme_location === 'primary'` replaces its DB-backed menu output outright. This is
  the one mechanism you identified, so it's one filter, not per-page special cases —
  it fires for every page that reaches GP's real header, however many of those there
  turn out to be after your cleanup lands.

  dropdown affordance worth building one-off) with the canonical Hebrew labels,
  untranslated, per team_00's ruling. `home` skipped there too (brand link covers it).
  The tree's own `apply_filters('ea_canonical_nav_items', $items)` is the extension
  point if a locale override is ever needed — data, not a second copy.
