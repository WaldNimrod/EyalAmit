# S007 M-04 · Accessibility re-check against the locked type scale

## Headline counts (as requested)

- **Genuine contrast failures, size-aware, across 12 pages: 193** (of 1,845 text elements
  measured), grouping to **41 distinct root causes** by (component, colour pair) — most of
  the 193 are the same handful of CSS rules repeating across pages or list items, not 193
  independent bugs. Full breakdown in Part 1.
- **False positives my own harness produced before I fixed it: at least 32**, in two batches,
  both caught and corrected before this report: **17** on a single-page dry run from a
  lazy-load/scroll-triggered-content trap (Methodology §1), and a further **15** on the full
  12-page run from a position:fixed-vs-sticky conflation (Methodology §2). Both are named,
  reproducible bugs in my *own* tooling, not guesses — see the worked examples below.
- **200% text resize:** 0 pages get horizontal scroll. Real (non-carousel, non-line-clamp)
  clipping containers exist on every page — see Part 2 — but none of the ones checked
  visually cut off text a visitor would otherwise read; several are pre-existing at 100%
  zoom too, not new at 200%.
- **Heading structure: clean on all 12 pages.** Exactly one `h1` each, zero skipped levels,
  zero `h1`→`h3` jumps.
- **Accessibility statement:** one claim (B5, "2x text enlargement with no content cut off")
  is **contradicted** by Part 2's measurements. One claim (B1, heading structure) is
  **confirmed**. Full claim-by-claim recheck in Part 4.

## Methodology — two false-positive traps caught in my own harness, and how

A contrast harness that reads background from the DOM was already known-bad going into this
(the mandate's own dry run: 12 false failures from `backgroundColor` being `transparent` over
gradients/images/video). My approach instead: for each page, hide every text run's ink
(`color:transparent; text-shadow:none`, no layout change) and screenshot the resulting
backdrop-only page, so the exact pixels behind each text element can be sampled directly —
no DOM colour resolution at all. Full method, with a worked example, in §3 below. But this
approach hit two of its own traps before it produced trustworthy numbers, both real, both
fixed before any measurement went into this report:

**§1 — Below-the-fold content doesn't render in a single non-scrolling screenshot.** First
dry run on the home page alone: 19 failures, most reading `ratio≈1.0`, `bg≈rgb(255,255,250)`
— near-white, for elements like `.cmpc__t` and `.studio__h` that are white text over real
photographs. Cropping the exact pixel region from both the normal and the backdrop-only
screenshot showed **both were blank cream** — the photo backgrounds for these
scroll-triggered (`ea-entrance`-class) sections simply hadn't rendered yet, because
`captureBeyondViewport` composites the whole page without the page ever having been
scrolled, and this site's entrance content needs a real scroll to arrive. Fix: scroll the
full page in 700px steps before enumerating or screenshotting, then return to the top. After
the fix, the same crop showed the real photograph (hands on a wooden didgeridoo, foliage) and
the same elements produced real, page-specific ratios (2.4–4.4) instead of a uniform false
"1.0". **17 of the 19 first-pass failures were this artifact**, confirmed by direct crop
comparison, not inferred.

**§2 — `position:fixed` and `position:sticky` are not the same thing for this purpose.** Once
§1 was fixed, the full 12-page run still produced implausible results: every nav link on the
home page read `bg≈rgb(255,255,250)` again (the nav is `position:fixed`, `chapters.css:119`
— sampling it from the huge scrolled-through composite picked up whatever unrelated content
sits at that pixel position further down the page, not the nav's own backdrop), and every FAQ
page table-of-contents link read a different, unrelated-looking colour
(`rgb(85,106,32)`, `rgb(35,35,69)`, `rgb(97,97,112)` …) — `.ea-faq-toc` is `position:sticky`
(`faq-toc.css:21`), and a sticky element's position at scroll-0 is wherever it naturally sits
in document flow, not pinned to the viewport top the way `fixed` is; treating it like `fixed`
sampled it against whatever else happened to occupy that (x,y) at the top of the page. Fix:
sample only true `position:fixed` ancestors from a dedicated viewport-sized screenshot taken
at real scroll-0 (also re-forcing this site's own `nav.setAttribute('data-s',...)`
scroll-state flag back to its resting value, since a synthetic scroll-then-return in headless
Chrome sometimes leaves it stuck "scrolled"); `sticky` elements are sampled normally, from
their real position in the full-page composite. **15 fewer failures** on the full 12-page run
after this fix (208 → 193), all confirmed as this pattern by inspecting the affected
selectors' CSS `position`.

### §3 — worked example, end to end

- **Element:** `.hero__trust` on `/`, `chapters.css:165` — "אייל עמית · פועל מאז 1999 …"
- **Rendered size/weight:** 13.6px, weight 500 → not large text → **4.5:1** required.
- **Foreground:** `getComputedStyle(el).color` = `rgba(255,255,255,0.93)`.
- **Backdrop sampling:** element rect `{x:?, y:? , w, h}` on the backdrop-only screenshot
  (text hidden, video given 1.2s to decode a real frame first — confirmed via
  `video.currentTime > 0 && video.readyState === 4` before capture); a 4×4 grid of points
  sampled inside the rect with a 3px margin (to avoid the box's own edge); darkest sampled
  pixel `rgb(77,84,41)` (dark olive, a shadowed leaf), lightest `rgb(190,186,150)`ish
  (sunlit foliage) region.
- **Ratio:** the semi-transparent white composited onto the darkest sampled pixel gives the
  worse-case ratio; computed **7.22:1** against the darkest sample in this run (an earlier,
  pre-scroll-through dry run without full page settle read a less favourable but also less
  correct 2.94 — the number moved because the page hadn't finished laying out, not because
  the true value is unstable).
- **Verdict:** PASS, well clear of 4.5:1, no near-miss.
- **Method label recorded in the CSV for every row:** "backdrop-only screenshot (text ink
  hidden via `color:transparent`), 4×4 grid sample per element, darkest-vs-lightest kept,
  WCAG relative-luminance ratio against the worse extreme."

Root font size used for all `rem`→px math: **16px**, confirmed live via
`getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).fontSize` (not assumed).

## Part 1 — Contrast, size-aware (41 root causes behind 193 failures)

Ordered by how likely a visitor is to hit them (repeat count × page spread), not by ratio.

### Real content, not placeholders

1. **FAQ answer chevrons, `/faq/` only — 118 occurrences, ratio ≈4.2 vs 4.5 required.**
   `.ea-faq-item__summary>span.ea-faq-item__icon` ("▾"), terracotta `rgb(181,102,61)` on
   near-white `rgb(255,255,250)`. One shared CSS rule; fixing it once fixes all 118. Not
   large text (17px/500 < 24px).

   `rgb(14,9,5)` — ratio **4.496**, i.e. 0.004 below the 4.5 line. This is the closest
   possible miss; whether it counts as failing depends on rounding convention, but by the
   literal WCAG number it is under.
3. **Accordion/FAQ body text on `/lessons/` and `/treatment/`.**
   `.dd__body p`, `rgb(103,72,45)` (brown) on backdrops ranging `rgb(33,20,10)` to

   paragraph — ratios from **1.5 to 2.2**, the most severe non-placeholder findings in this
   report. Same component, same colour pair, different photograph per instance — this is a

   `.bleed__a` ("חיה עזריה", "אייל עמית"), `rgb(208,138,94)` (tan) on `rgb(127,146,114)`

   instances: `.chap` on `/contact/` ("צור קשר", ratio 1.4) and `/accessibility/` ("משפטי",

   1.07) and `/contact/` ("קשר", ratio 1.9, and this one **is** large text at 44px/300 so
   its bar is only 3:1 — still fails). All the same shape of problem: a fixed terracotta/tan
   accent colour, chosen to read well over one hero image, sitting over a different one.
6. **Nav links on interior ("phero") pages, borderline.**
   `.nav__l a` / `.nav__dd`, white 82% opacity over each page's own hero image — ratios

## Part 2 — 200% text resize

**No page produces horizontal scroll at 200%** (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth` ==
`clientWidth` on all 12, checked directly, not inferred from an unrelated reflow number).

**Real clipping containers** (ancestor's own computed `overflow` is `hidden`/`clip` in that
axis AND that ancestor's own `scrollHeight`/`scrollWidth` exceeds its `clientHeight`/
`clientWidth` — the mandate's own distinction, applied literally; horizontal carousels
(`.testi-mq__viewport`, transform-sliding by design) and any `-webkit-line-clamp` truncation
are excluded up front as intentional, not bugs):

- **`header.phero.phero--media` — every interior page with this hero, `scrollWidth 1660` vs
  `clientWidth 1440` (+220px), both before and after 200% resize (not a resize regression —
  present at 100% too).** Walked every descendant inside `.phero` looking for one wider than
  1440px and found none — no single text-bearing element is itself overflowing. This reads
  as a structural/decorative sizing issue (most likely a background media element sized to
  its own intrinsic dimensions rather than the container), not a text-clipping one, but I did
  not fully trace the exact overflowing node given the mandate's scope is text/typography —
  flagged as **COULD NOT FULLY RESOLVE**, worth a follow-up if it matters to layout QA.
- **`footer.foot` — every page, `scrollWidth 1590` vs `clientWidth 1440` (+150px)**, same
  pattern: present at 100% zoom already, not new at 200%, no text-bearing descendant found
  wider than the container.
- **`section.cta-band.cta-band--row` — `/`, `/lessons/`, `/books/`, `/books/vekatavta/`
  only, Y-axis, present at both zoom levels.** Content-length-dependent: pages whose CTA
  copy is longer overflow a height that pages with shorter copy don't. This is the kind of
  clipping the mandate's home-hero fix (1.5.47) already addressed for the hero specifically;
  this is a *different* section with the same shape of risk, not yet touched.
- **`div.studio__t.r` (home page only)** — `scrollHeight` exceeds `clientHeight` by ~120px on
  a `display:flex; flex-direction:column; justify-content:center; overflow:hidden` box
  (`chapters.css:228`) — centering means any overflow clips symmetrically at *both* the top
  and bottom edges, not just the bottom, which makes it easy to miss on a casual look. A
  direct screenshot crop of this section (normal render, not backdrop-only) shows the
  heading and all three paragraphs fully legible with no obviously missing line — **I could
  not conclusively determine from a screenshot alone whether ~60px is being trimmed
  symmetrically from top and bottom padding or from the text itself.** Reporting the
  measured numbers and the mechanism plainly; this one needs a human eye on the actual
  rendered page, not a further round of my own inference.
- **`div.mokesh-hero__yt` (Mokesh memorial page only)** — a YouTube embed container,
  overflowing both axes. Almost certainly an iframe/aspect-ratio mismatch specific to that
  one embed, not a typography-scale effect; noted for completeness, not chased further
  (video embeds are explicitly out of typography scope and are also disclaimed in the
  accessibility statement itself — see Part 4, D2).
- **`div.screen-reader-response` (Contact page)** — Contact Form 7's own ARIA live-region
  for post-submit messages, `scrollHeight 51` vs `clientHeight 1`. This is a known,
  intentional CF7 pattern (the region is deliberately near-invisible until a submission
  triggers a message, and is meant for assistive-tech announcement, not visual display) —
  **not treated as a finding.**

## Part 3 — Heading structure

**Clean on all 12 pages.** Exactly one visible `h1` per page (confirmed:
`document.querySelectorAll('h1,h2,...')` filtered to actually-rendered headings, not just
present-in-DOM), zero skipped levels, and specifically zero `h1`→`h3` jumps despite the new
`h3`-weight tier (`--fw-h3:600`, `ea-tokens.css:61`) now existing in the stylesheet — it is
applied to `h3`-tagged elements correctly, not used as a same-level substitute anywhere that
would break the sequence.

## Part 4 — The accessibility statement, claim by claim

Re-measured against the current site, not read and trusted. Full extraction of every claim
on the page is available on request; the ones this mandate's scope can actually re-verify:

- **B1 ("proper heading structure, one H1, no skipped levels") — CONFIRMED**, independently,
  by Part 3 above, on all 12 pages in this mandate's set (the statement doesn't name which
  pages it checked — its own text says "twelve main pages," matching this mandate's own page
  count, though not verified to be the *same* twelve).
- **B5 ("text enlarges up to 2× with no content cut off and no horizontal scrolling") —
  PARTIALLY CONTRADICTED.** The no-horizontal-scroll half holds exactly as claimed (Part 2).
  The no-content-cut-off half does not: Part 2 found real `overflow:hidden` containers whose
  content exceeds their box on every page in this set, present at 100% zoom already and thus
  necessarily still present at 200%. Whether any of them are cutting off *readable text*
  specifically (as opposed to decorative excess) is not fully resolved for two of them (see
  Part 2's own hedging on `.phero`/`.footer`/`.studio__t`) — but the blanket claim "no content
  cut off" cannot be made at face value while these are open questions, and the mandate's own
  framing (this statement "has already had to be corrected three times") means this is not a
  claim to extend the benefit of the doubt to.
- **B7 ("colour contrast adapted for text, including keyboard-focus state") — NOT FULLY
  SUPPORTED by Part 1.** The statement doesn't claim every single instance passes, but "color
  contrast adapted" reads as a general assurance, and Part 1 found 193 real instances (41
  root causes) below the size-aware threshold, several severely (ratio ~1.2–1.5). The
  *focus-state* half of this claim was not measured under this mandate (no keyboard-focus
  contrast pass was run) — **COULD NOT MEASURE**, not confirmed or denied.
- **B0/C0/C5's own claimed measurement scope** ("tested on the site itself in September
  2026, across twelve main pages") is itself unverifiable from outside — I have no way to
  confirm which twelve pages the statement's authors used, or that "September 2026" refers
  to a check against 1.5.48 specifically rather than an earlier September build. Flagged as
  **COULD NOT VERIFY**, not as false.
- **A3 (IS 5568 / WCAG 2.0 AA), A4/A5 (no external audit, no full-conformance claim), C1
  (no screen-reader testing), C2 (some book-gallery images still undescribed), C3 (focus-ring
  prominence), C4 (third-party video not fully controlled), D1–D3 (video/caption
  disclaimers), E1–E3 (contact mechanism), F1 (last-updated date)** — all outside this
  mandate's typography/contrast/resize/heading scope; not re-measured here. None of them
  contradict anything this mandate did check.
