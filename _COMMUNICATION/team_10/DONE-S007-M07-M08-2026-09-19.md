## M-08 — three controls a keyboard can reach and an eye cannot find (theme 1.5.74)

**1. Closed Wave2 drawer — FIXED, verified with real Tab, not `.focus()` loops.**
`.ea-mnav-drawer` computed `display:flex;visibility:visible;opacity:1`, held off-screen
by `transform` alone. Measured before: 9 of 15 real-Tab stops from `BODY` on `/about/`
(390px) landed inside it, at negative x. Copied the exact pattern already used for the
Chapters burger (`chapters.css .nav__l`, WS-2.2/A11Y-FIX-2026-09-18) rather than inventing
a second mechanism: `visibility:hidden` on close, delayed by the slide duration so the
close animation is unaffected; `visibility:visible` with zero delay on open. Re-tested
live, real key events, all four required combinations:

| Page | Viewport | Closed-drawer tab hits |
|---|---|---:|
| `/about/` | 390 | 9/15 -> **0/15** |
| `/press/` | 390 | (not separately measured before) -> **0/20** |
| `/about/` | 1440 | -> **0/20** |
| `/press/` | 1440 | -> **0/20** |

Real Escape still closes it (unaffected by this change, checked anyway).

**2. Background "not inert" when open — COULD NOT REPRODUCE, not fixed.**
The mandate's evidence: `main.click()` reached the page behind the open drawer, and there
is no scroll lock. I re-tested this live, twice (before touching anything, and again after
the drawer-visibility fix), with real interaction rather than a synthetic call:
- **Scroll lock:** `.ea-mnav-open` and `.ea-mnav-open body` already compute
  `overflow:hidden` (this rule already exists in `ea-mobile-nav.css`, lines 207-209 — I
  did not add it). A real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` wheel event at the drawer's open state
  left `window.scrollY` unchanged in my first test run. A second run (after the tab-walk
  fix, where focus now legitimately reaches content much further down the page before the
  drawer is opened) showed scrollY moving under a wheel event at a fixed test coordinate —
  I could not tell whether that coordinate was landing inside the drawer's own scrollable
  list rather than the page, and did not want to report a regression I could not pin down.
  `overflow:hidden` is confirmed present and unchanged by my commit either way.
- **Click-reach:** `document.elementFromPoint()` at multiple coordinates across the full
  390px viewport while the drawer is open returned `.ea-mnav-scrim` every time, never
  `main` — the scrim (`pointer-events:auto`, confirmed) sits on top and would catch a real
  click anywhere on screen.
- I did not add `inert` or convert to `<dialog>`. Both options in the mandate ("Chapters
  sets `nav-locked`" / native `<dialog>`) would be fixing something I could not confirm is
  broken by real interaction — `nav-locked` itself is only ever `overflow:hidden`, which
  this drawer already has under a different class name. If `main.click()` in the original
  finding was a direct programmatic call rather than a real click at those coordinates,
  that call bypasses hit-testing entirely and would fire regardless of the scrim — which
  would explain the disagreement without either finding being wrong. Reporting this as a
  discrepancy rather than guessing which reproduction was used.

**3. Five FAQ chips off-screen — FIXED, verified across all 16 chips, not a sample.**
`.ea-faq-toc` is a 2188px-wide horizontal scroller in a 388px viewport at <=560px
(measured live). Added a `focus` listener in `ea-faq-toc.js`:
`a.scrollIntoView({inline:'nearest', block:'nearest'})` — scrolls only the strip, never
the page. Re-tested: tabbed through all 16 chips on `/faq/` at 390px; every single one
reports `left >= 0` and `right <= 390` at the moment it receives focus (before the fix,
6+ were at negative `left`, matching the mandate's named ones).

**What NOT to do, honored:** did not touch anything hiding a control's accessible name —
no changes near the TOC rail labels.
