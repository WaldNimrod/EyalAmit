# M-09 — the two Yoni-page quotes as cards beside the story

Deployed (commit `e3b56ea`, theme 1.5.76). Verified against the live site after deploy,
not against local files. Validation is team_50's, on a different engine.

## What was built

**New generic part**, `template-parts/chapters/parts/testi-cards.php` — renders any number
of quote `<blockquote>`s from `$args['quotes']` (an array of HTML strings). No title,
attribution, rating, date or label of its own; renders nothing if the array is empty.

**Pairing**: `split.php` gained a `pairs_with_cards` arg, mirroring `prose.php`'s existing
`pairs_with_toc` — sets a marker class (`ea-cards-lede-lead`) on the section. `chapters.css`
gained one more `display:contents` + `:has()` pairing block, the same technique already used
for `.ea-toc-lede-lead` (reused, not reinvented, per the mandate — see the comment beside it
for why it can't be a nested `:has()`, same reason as the existing one). Side by side above
861px, stacked below.

**Design**: `.ea-testi-cards__card` — `var(--fs-sm)` (15.3px), `var(--fw-light)` (300),
`var(--bf)` (Heebo). No new size or weight value; both are existing rungs from canon §1. No
`<button>` rendered by this part, so the "set font-family explicitly on any button" note in
the mandate did not apply here.

**Content**: the two quotes were relocated character for character out of the inline
`<blockquote>` elements in `snoring-sleep-apnea-defaults.php`'s Yoni section, into the new
`testi-cards` section placed immediately after it:
- `<strong>"בדיקת השינה לא הדגימה תסמונת הפסקות נשימה בשינה."</strong>`
- `<strong>"ב-2014 היו לי 250 הפסקות נשימה בלילה."</strong>`

No wording changed, no attribution/heading/rating/date added (none existed in the source).

## Checked, not assumed: the mandate's "prose still reads correctly" claim

The mandate says removing the quotes "leaves that prose still reading correctly" and asks me
to check that, with an explicit instruction to stop and report rather than rewrite if it
doesn't. **It does not, fully.** Two sentences in the surrounding prose were written
specifically to introduce the quotes:

> ...וכשהוא קיבל את התוצאות, הוא שלח לי הודעה. **[quote 1 was here]** מיד אחר כך הוא כתב:
> **[quote 2 was here]** אני זוכר את ההתרגשות...

With the quotes relocated, "מיד אחר כך הוא כתב:" ("Right after, he wrote:") now ends on a
colon with nothing following it before the next sentence starts a new thought. This is
visible on the live page today (confirmed: the sentence is present verbatim in the rendered
text). I did not rewrite it — the mandate's content law forbids adding words that are not
already in the source file, and rewriting Eyal's own sentence to smooth the transition would
be exactly that. Flagging it here for Eyal or team_00 to decide: options I can see without
choosing one myself are (a) leave it — a reader can likely still follow it with the card
sitting right beside the paragraph, (b) drop those two lead-in sentences entirely since their
only job was introducing quotes that no longer follow them inline, or (c) some other rewording
that only Eyal's own words should supply.
