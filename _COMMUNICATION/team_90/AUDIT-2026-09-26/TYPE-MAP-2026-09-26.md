The cookie dialog was closed, scroll-reveal nodes (`.r`) were brought to the revealed state, then two animation frames were waited. Viewport time is 2026-09-26 evening, Asia/Jerusalem.

`text-align: start` on an RTL page means the text sits on the physical right. Physical left/right below are viewport coordinates, not writing-mode.

## Four things to raise first

1. **The CTA the owner described is what 1.5.138 actually draws, and it is still not a settled type.** At 1440 the band is three equal tracks of about 357px: logo in the right third, text right-aligned in the middle third, button centred in the left third. At 390 it collapses to one column (small centred mark, then text, then button). That matches the owner’s sentence. It does not match the 23 Sep canon, which still names `cta-band--choc` + `cta-band--stack` (zero live uses; the chocolate fill is marked retired in the CSS). What varies, and will matter when someone asks for “a CTA with content B”, is the **content shape**: of 29 bands on 19 pages, only 8 have both a heading and a paragraph. The rest are body-only, heading-only, or a button sitting in an empty middle third. Thirteen pages add `cta-band--sand`. `/books/` uses an outline button (`btn--gw`) instead of the terracotta default.

2. **The eight “join” patterns from 23 Sep are real, and almost all of them live on one page.** Compact hero (78vh), standing float, cover split, point cards, portrait gallery, photo band, card-style FAQ, and the sand pill are on `/repair/` and, except the sand pill’s CSS, nowhere else. Every other inner page with a photograph uses the 88vh media hero, not the 78vh compact one the canon called the internal default.

3. **Two large populations are outside the row system.** All 52 published posts are a photo hero plus `.ea-post-content` (whatever `the_content` stored). None of them use the JSON row mapper in `ea-blog-json.php`. All 48 `/qr/qrN/` articles are a text hero plus one `.intro-body` of migrated HTML. “A row of type A on this post” has no type to point at.

4. **`block-topnav.php` is the orphan the brief predicted, and it is not alone.** It is called from three legacy templates; a later `template_include` filter serves Chapters templates instead, and the class `ea-topnav` is on zero of the 136 pages. The same fate meets `mag.php`, `steps.php`, `lead.php`, `reveals.php`, `product-cta.php`, and most of the Wave 2 block partials.

## What a canon page can take as stable

For a type the owner can order by name, the stable contract is the **inputs** column, not the current paint of the CTA.
